Motion & accessibility

How things move, and the rules that always apply.
  • Fast 150ms hovers and presses · base 250ms theme and panels · slow 400ms the window sliding.
  • Easing: ease-out for things arriving, ease-in-out for things moving across.
  • The visualizer animates between steps: the window capsule slides, the incoming letter lifts, the bubble text crossfades.
  • With reduced motion on: no tilts, no slides. States just switch.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA. Text 4.5:1, large text and UI edges 3:1, in both themes.
  • Touch targets at least 44px. Visible focus ring on everything.
  • Never colour alone: pair it with a label, border style or icon.
  • Every page: one h1, landmarks, skip link, breadcrumb below top level.
