About pointer

Pointer teaches the 15 patterns behind most coding interviews by showing them move, one step at a time. Watch a one-minute animation, drive the visualizer yourself, then solve the real problems.

[PHOTO]
Made by Rohan Software engineer. Builds the animations in code, and learns best by seeing things move.

Patterns, not answers

Memorised solutions fall apart in interviews. Recognising the shape of a problem doesn’t.

Free, no account

Everything is open. Your progress stays in your own browser.

Made in code

Explainers are animated with Manim, the open-source library created for 3Blue1Brown. Visualizers run the real algorithm.

Accessibility

Pointer aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Found something that gets in your way? Tell me at [EMAIL] and I’ll fix it.

  • Keyboard first. Every control works with Tab, arrows, Space and Enter. Press / to search.
  • Screen readers. The visualizer announces every step in words.
  • Captions and transcripts on every explainer.
  • Your pace. Slow the visualizer down, or turn on reduced motion to keep things still.
  • Light and dark themes, following your system setting.
  • Never colour alone. States are also shown with shapes, labels and borders.
