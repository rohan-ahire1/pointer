Sliding Window

Look out of a train window. New scenery slides in on the right, old scenery slides out on the left, and you never re-read the whole view.

⏱ ~15 min Needs: arrays, sets O(n)

Reach for it when…

  • the answer is a contiguous run
  • it asks for the longest, shortest or a count
  • brute force is two nested loops
